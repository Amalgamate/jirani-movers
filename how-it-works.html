Before moving day

Six things that make it smoother.

1

Sort firstGo through each room and decide what stays, goes or gets donated. Less to move costs less.

2

Keep essentials with youID, documents, jewellery, chargers and medicine travel with you, not on the truck.

3

Tell us the tricky partsStairs, lifts, narrow gates and parking all change the plan. Mention them when you call.

4

Empty the fridgeClear and defrost the fridge and freezer a day before so they are ready to move.

5

Set aside a first-night bagPack toiletries, a change of clothes and bedding where you can find them first.

6

Point out fragile itemsShow the team anything delicate or valuable so it gets extra attention.
